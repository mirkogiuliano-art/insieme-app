You must be **at least 16 years old** to use Insieme.

- other people's personal data without their consent;
- malicious software, or attempts to tamper with how the service works.

Groups are private and invite-only: that doesn't make acceptable inside them
what wouldn't be acceptable anywhere else.

## Reporting and blocking

From any message you can **report** it to whoever runs the service, or **block**
the person who wrote it. Blocking applies only to you: it hides their messages
in your chats and the other person is not told.

Reports are read and assessed. If some content breaks these terms we can remove
it and, in serious or repeated cases, suspend or close the account of whoever
published it. We have no obligation to monitor in advance what people write to
each other in their private groups.

## Leaving

You can delete your account whenever you like from your *Profile* in the app,
without asking anyone's permission. What disappears and what stays is
explained in the privacy policy.

We can suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, or discontinue the
service: in that case we will give reasonable notice, where possible.
