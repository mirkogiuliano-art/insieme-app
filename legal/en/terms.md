From any message you can **report** it to whoever runs the service, or **block**

each other; the other person is not told.

Reports are read and assessed by a person, not by an automated system. If some
content breaks these terms or the law we can remove it and, in serious or
repeated cases, suspend or close the account of whoever published it. A public
group that breaks them can be made private again. We have no obligation to
monitor in advance what people write to each other in their private groups.

**People who don't use Insieme** can also report illegal content to us by
writing to **[contact email address]**: say where it is and why you believe it

If we remove your content, make one of your groups private again or restrict

ask us to reconsider the decision by writing to the same address. You can always
go to court as well. If we become aware of content suggesting a crime that
